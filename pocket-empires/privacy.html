Privacy Policy

Pocket Empires, by Pocket Agent Games

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Pocket Empires is an offline, single-player game. It is designed to run on your device. This policy explains how we handle information.

What we collect

We do not collect personal data. The game does not ask you to create an account, and it does not require you to sign in.

We do not run analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services in the current version of the game, and we do not sell or share personal data with third parties, because we do not collect it.

Data that stays on your device

Game saves and settings are stored locally on your device so you can continue a playthrough. That information stays on the device unless you choose to copy or back it up yourself. Uninstalling the game may delete it.

Internet access

Pocket Empires is designed to be played offline. The current version does not need a network connection to play.

Children

The game is a strategy sandbox and is not directed at children under 13. Because we do not collect personal data, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the new version at this page. If we ever start collecting data, we will say so clearly here before that happens.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or the game, email us at pocketagentgamesupport@gmail.com.
